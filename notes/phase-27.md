## On "complete in order"

`portfolio-build.md`'s own Phase 0 learning rules say to complete phases
in order "unless a step explicitly says otherwise." Phase 23 is the one
deliberate deviation in this run, and it was named and reasoned about
up front (in the plan, before writing any code) rather than drifted into
midway through. Every other phase in 15-29 ran in the order listed
above.
